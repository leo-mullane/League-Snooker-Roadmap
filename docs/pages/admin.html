Prototype 5 of 5 · Run a league · Admin

What we're proposing

A secretary's week on one screen: a fast entry grid built from the fixture list (type the frame scores, Enter moves on, the row checks itself), a queue of captains' submissions to approve or query, helpers with their own sign-in and exports of everything.

Why

The site says a week's results for four divisions typically take an hour to enter. With captains submitting from their phones the secretary's job becomes checking rather than typing, and the grid makes the typing that remains quicker. The Join page already suggests passing match cards to someone else when the secretary is short of time; a helper role makes that official, with their own password.

Type frame scores into the grid (they must total 10), approve the waiting submission, invite a helper or export a file.
