Prototype 2 of 5 · League hub · Past seasons

What we're proposing

The current season by default everywhere, and an archive page with a card for each past season: who won each division, the highest break and a link that opens that season's tables, results and breaks.

Why

Cambridge & District has 21 seasons online back to 2005/06, which is a remarkable record. At present every league page carries a dropdown with all 21 at the top, which is a lot of history to scroll past to reach this week's table, and nothing says who won what.

Covers idea 22. Season dates are from the live site; the winners shown are sample data.
