Prototype 1 of 5 · Community home · Championship

What we're proposing

A proper home for the UK League Snooker Championship: dates and venue, how a team qualifies and enters, the groups and draw on the weekend, results as they come in and every past winner.

Why

The championship is the one event that brings the leagues on the site together, and at the moment it is a single line on the homepage linking to a page laid out like any other league table. It deserves to be the thing a secretary forwards to their players.

Covers idea 15. Groups, entrants and past winners below are placeholders.
