Prototype 2 of 5 · League hub · Fixtures and results

What we're proposing

Fixtures grouped by week with the actual match night as the heading, a jump to this week, a filter to show one team and a calendar feed so a player's fixtures sit in their own phone calendar and move when the fixture moves. Cup weeks show in the sequence so gaps make sense.

Why

Division 1's season is currently one list of 48 rows. The weekly results page is headed with the Monday of the week ("Monday, September 21") although the matches were played on Friday 25 September, which is the sort of thing that makes people double-check everything else.

Weeks 1 to 3 of Division 1 are the real results; later fixtures are sample data.
