Foundations · applies to every page

What we're proposing

The groundwork that sits under all five prototypes and that nobody sees when it is done properly: one site that works on any screen, readable type, proper headings and page titles, accessibility to WCAG 2.2 AA, new addresses with redirects from every old one, and a clean-up of the data as it moves across.

Why

The current site is built as it would have been in the mid-2000s: an HTML 4.01 page, no viewport setting, a 970-pixel layout in tables, 12-pixel Verdana and a separate copy of the site for phones. None of it is broken exactly, but it is why the site is hard to use on a phone and close to invisible to search engines. Rebuilding is cheaper than retrofitting all of it.

The design system is applied across the whole pack from a single stylesheet.

One site for every screen

One responsive site from 320 pixels up, with the same address on a phone and a laptop. The /mobile/ copy and its redirects are retired. Every prototype in this pack works at phone width; try any of them on your phone.

The League Snooker design system (colour, type and components) is applied from a single stylesheet, so a change to the brand is made once rather than page by page. Where we don't yet have photography, the images are snooker tables drawn to scale.

Now

Verdana 12px, used for everything including results tables.

Proposed

17px body text, 15px in dense tables, figures that line up in columns and 48px targets for anything you tap.

Page titles and headings

Every page has one H1 and a title that says what and whose it is. These are what appear in search results and browser tabs.

ContentEvery page also gets its own description, rather than the one shared by every page today ("The Snooker League Website for any Snooker UK League, online snooker, snooker pictures, snooker forum"). League pages carry structured data for events and sports teams.

Accessibility

  • WCAG 2.2 AA as the minimum, tested with a screen reader and keyboard before launch.
  • Tables with captions and scoped headers; form and results given in words as well as symbols.
  • Alt text for every image (none of the five images on the rankings page has any today).
  • Visible focus, no hover-only controls and everything usable with a keyboard. Escape closes every overlay.
  • Colour is never the only signal: a win, a draw and a loss differ in shape as well as colour.

Addresses and redirects

New addresses read like the site: /leagues/cambridge-district-snooker/division-1/table. Every old address gets a permanent redirect, so bookmarks, links in club newsletters and search rankings carry over.

Try: · · ·

ContentSome current menu labels point at pages with unrelated addresses ("Privacy" opens snooker-software.asp, "Links" opens snooker-results.asp). The new addresses match what the page is.

Advertising

Open programmatic advertising is replaced by one curated sponsor slot per page, chosen by the league or the community. Today's pages ask for consent twice, the second time on behalf of 210 advertising partners; without open advertising that second request disappears.
