Prototype 1 of 5 · Community home

What we're proposing

A homepage that starts with one search box. Type a league, a club, a team, a player or the first half of a postcode and the results appear as you type, grouped by kind. Underneath it the page splits into two routes, one for players and one for league secretaries, followed by what has happened across the community this week, the championship and the most recent centuries.

Why

Today the homepage is an alphabetical list of 87 leagues beside a map of the UK, and search is three separate searches (people by last name only). If you already know which league you play in that works well enough. If you don't, or you are a secretary wondering whether to move your league over, there is very little to go on. The proposition is currently a numbered list of five purposes and one testimonial of more than 400 words.

The league finder and the championship page are linked from here.

Every UK snooker and billiards league in one place
