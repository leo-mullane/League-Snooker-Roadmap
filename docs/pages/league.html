Prototype 2 of 5 · League hub · Overview

What we're proposing

Each league gets a home page that leads with what players come for on a league night: this week's fixtures, last week's results as scoreboards, the top of the table and the best breaks. Eight tabs take you to everything else. The league can add its own logo and sponsor, and the secretary is reached through a form rather than a published email address.

Why

On a phone, the Cambridge & District table currently starts around 700 pixels down, below the site navigation, the administrator's email address, an eighteen-link menu, the cups row and an advert. On a desktop the page is fixed at 970 pixels wide. Nothing in the data needs to change for this; it is a question of what goes first.

Covers ideas 8, 9, 19, 20, 22, 28 and 47. Try "Follow a team": the team then comes first in this week's fixtures and is highlighted in every table.
