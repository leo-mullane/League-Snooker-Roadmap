Prototype 1 of 5 · Community home · League finder

What we're proposing

A league finder that works from a postcode, a town or "near me", with a map and a list that stay in step. Filters cover the things people choose a league by: snooker or billiards, the night it plays, team or singles and whether it is a handicap, veterans or summer league.

Why

Those details are already there, but only inside league names ("Thursday", "Veterans", "Summer", "Handicap"), so the only way to find a Thursday league near Southport is to read all 87 names. The current map is an image with 65 clickable areas, which doesn't work on a phone and can't be searched. Storing these as fields also means a league's page can say "Fridays, East of England" without anyone typing it.

The map is a placeholder here; the build would use a real map with clustered pins.
