Prototype 3 of 5 · Match centre · Live scoring

What we're proposing

Optional live scoring: someone at the table taps the balls as they go down and anyone following sees the frame score, the break in progress and the match score update. It would start with cup finals and the championship rather than every league night.

Why

Live scoring is now normal in the amateur game, including in the governing body's own tournament tools. It is not something every league will want on a Friday night in a working men's club, which is why we suggest it as an option for the occasions where people who can't be there want to follow.

Try the ball buttons, then "Miss" and "End frame".
