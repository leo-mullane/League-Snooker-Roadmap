Prototype 4 of 5 · Players · Profile

What we're proposing

One profile per player that follows them across seasons, teams and leagues: frames won this season and over a career, form by season, highest breaks and centuries, handicap history and head to head against anyone they have played. Players can follow each other and can choose how their name appears.

Why

A player page today covers one season: won, drawn, lost and a list of matches. The site has held these records since 2005, but they can't be seen together. Handicaps are often typed into the name itself ("Matt Wilson -55", "Matt Wilson K"), so the same person appears several times. Separating the handicap from the name and merging those records is the work that makes a career view possible.

Covers ideas 34, 35 and 38. Player names and figures are sample data. Try the head to head with a different opponent.
