Prototype 5 of 5 · Run a league · Start your league

What we're proposing

A guided setup that asks the same things the current Join page asks, grouped into five short steps and ends with a preview of the league's new site. The secretary chooses their own password on screen.

Why

Today a new league answers 21 numbered questions by email, the last of which is "Administrator Username and Password". Asking anyone to email a password is a habit worth breaking, and a form can explain each option ("does your league record frame scores, e.g. John Smith 45 v 76 Paul Jones?") at the point it matters rather than in a list.

Every one of the 21 current questions is covered in steps 1 to 3.
