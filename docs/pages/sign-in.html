Prototype 5 of 5 · Run a league · Sign in

What we're proposing

A sign-in page with a working "Forgotten your password?" route, so a secretary can get back in without emailing anyone.

Why

The current admin log-in is a username and password box with nothing else. If a secretary forgets their details, or hands the league over, the only route is to ask.

Any email and password will do here.

Secretary sign in

Forgotten your password?

Enter the email you sign in with and we'll send a link to choose a new password. The link works for one hour.

Check your email. If that address has an account, a link is on its way. Nothing arrived? Your league's other admins can also reset it for you.
