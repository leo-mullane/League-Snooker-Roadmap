Prototype 3 of 5 · Match centre · Submit a result

What we're proposing

Home captains submit the result from their phone at the end of the night, in four steps: which match, the frames, the breaks and a photo of the paper match card. The away captain is asked to confirm it, and it goes live once they do, or after 48 hours if nobody queries it. The secretary sees everything in one queue and only steps in when there's a query.

Why

The site already has a "Submit Result" link on unplayed fixtures, so the idea is established. Most of the secretary's hour a week goes on typing up match cards that someone else has already written out once. Moving that to the people holding the card, with a confirmation from the other side, takes the bulk of it away without losing the check.

Covers idea 31, and feeds the secretary's queue in idea 43. Try it with the real outstanding fixture, JP's v Newton 1 from 2 October.
