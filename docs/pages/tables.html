Prototype 2 of 5 · League hub · Tables

What we're proposing

Divisions switch with a single tap and the choice is remembered. Each table shows recent form and how far a team has moved since last week, and the team you follow is highlighted. On a phone the less-used columns drop away so nothing scrolls sideways.

Why

Divisions are picked today from a dropdown that reloads the page, and the table shows totals only. The numbers here are the real Division 1 table after week 3; the form and movement are worked out from the same results, so there is nothing extra for the secretary to enter.

Tables

AccessibilityEach table has a caption and scoped row and column headers, so a screen reader announces "Whittlesford 2, F, 23" rather than a string of numbers. Form is given as text ("Last 3: W, W, W") as well as the boxes.
Question for the clientCambridge & District ranks on frames won, so there is no points column. Leagues that use points will show a Pts column; is there any league whose tie-break rules we should check before build?
