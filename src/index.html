UK League Snooker

A rebuild of leaguesnooker.co.uk, in five templates

The UK League Snooker site has carried the results of 87 leagues for more than twenty years. These prototypes show how a rebuilt site could work, from finding a league to submitting a result on a Friday night. Everything you would expect to click does something.

The prototypes

  • Community home
    1

    Community home and league finder

    A homepage that starts with one search box for leagues, clubs, teams, players and postcodes, then splits into routes for players and secretaries. Includes the league finder with a map, the championship page and the forum archive.

    Try: type "Whittlesford" or "CB22" into the search, then open the league finder and filter to Thursday veterans leagues.

    Open prototype
  • League hub
    2

    League hub

    A league's own pages, using Cambridge & District and its real Division 1 results: this week, results as scoreboards, tables with form, fixtures by week, cups as brackets, club pages, past seasons and a contact form for the secretary.

    Try: "Follow a team", then look at the overview, the tables and the fixtures. Add your team's fixtures to a calendar.

    Open prototype
  • Match centre
    3

    Match centre

    A match card for every result, the captain's four-step result submission with confirmation from the other side and optional live scoring for finals.

    Try: submit the real outstanding result, JP's v Newton 1, and make the frames not add up.

    Open prototype
  • Players
    4

    Players

    A career profile across seasons and leagues with handicap history and head to head, rankings with a qualifying number of frames, the centuries hall of fame and a player's alerts and privacy settings.

    Try: change the head to head opponent, then switch your name to initials in alerts and privacy and look at a match card.

    Open prototype
  • Run a league
    5

    Run a league

    The secretary's side: how it works and a pricing calculator, a guided setup that replaces the 21 questions by email, sign-in with password reset and an admin area for entering and approving results, helpers and exports.

    Try: price a 20-team league, start a league and get to the preview, then enter scores in the admin grid.

    Open prototype
  • Foundations
    +

    Foundations

    What sits under every page: one responsive site, readable type, titles and headings, accessibility, new addresses with redirects from the old ones, consent and the data clean-up. Plus the module library.

    Try: paste an old leaguesnooker.co.uk address into the redirect checker.

    Open

How to review

  • This round applies the new League Snooker design system: baize greens for structure, chalk blue for anything you can click, and the seven ball colours kept for scores and statistics. Comments on the look are welcome now, as well as on what each page does.
  • The live site has almost no photography of its own, so most images are drawn as a snooker table in the brand colours. The championship venue photo comes from leaguesnooker.co.uk.
  • Turn Notes on (top right) to see what each page proposes and why, plus numbered notes: yellow for behaviour, red for questions we need you to answer.
  • League, team and club names are real, as are Cambridge & District's Division 1 results for the first three weeks. Player names and everything else are sample data.
  • Try them on your phone. They are built for it.
