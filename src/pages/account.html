What we're proposing

A light account for players: follow teams and players, choose how and when to hear about results and fixtures, claim your own player record and decide whether your full name or just your initial appears on public pages.

Why

The current Subscribe option sends the same league-wide email to everyone whenever the secretary presses the button. Most players care about one team and perhaps a couple of rivals. Following is also what lets every page put "your" team first, which you can see on the league hub once you have followed one.
