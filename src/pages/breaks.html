What we're proposing

A hall of fame for centuries across every league, filtered by season, league or player, with 147s at the top and a shareable card for each break. The same list, filtered to one league, is that league's Breaks tab.

Why

The all-league centuries page is one of the site's best features and one of its least visible. Because names carry handicaps, the same player appears under several spellings, and at least one season is listed as ending before it started (1 September 2023 to 30 April 2023). Clean player records fix both, and a century is the thing a player is most likely to share.
