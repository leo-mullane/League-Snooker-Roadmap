Prototype 2 of 5 · League hub · Clubs

What we're proposing

A page for every club: where it is, how to get there, a phone number you can tap, how many tables it has, which teams play there and what is on there this week.

Why

The clubs page today is a table of 18 names, postcodes and phone numbers. The postcodes open Google Maps, but none of the numbers can be tapped on a phone, and there is no way to see which teams a club is home to. For an away player it is the page they need on the night.

Club names, postcodes and numbers are from the live site; table counts are sample data.
