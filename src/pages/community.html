What we're proposing

Keep the community forum online as a read-only archive, searchable and with its old addresses still working, and point people to the Facebook group and to their own league's news for anything current.

Why

The newest post on the community forum is from March 2022. The conversation has moved to Facebook, which is fine, but a forum with a "New Topic" button and no replies for four years suggests the whole site has been left alone. The old threads (exhibitions, coaching, handicap systems) are still worth finding.
