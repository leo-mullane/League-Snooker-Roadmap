What we're proposing

One Cups tab for all of a league's competitions, with knockouts drawn as a bracket that fills in as results arrive, and the date and venue rules for the next round alongside.

Why

Cambridge & District runs four cups, each reached from its own link in a row under the main menu. A knockout is easier to follow as a bracket than as a list, and "who do we play next and where" is the question players actually ask.
