What we're proposing

Everything a league publishes that isn't results lives under "More": news, pictures, rules, documents, the roll of honour, FAQs, past seasons and the archived forum. Two new things sit here as well. A contact form that reaches the secretary without showing their address, and widgets the league or its clubs can paste into their own website or Facebook page.

Why

Seven of today's eighteen menu links end up here (pictures, roll of honour, news, FAQs, forum, documents and rules); search and subscribe become site-wide. They matter, but they are not what most people open the site for, so they move off the main row. The administrator's personal email address currently appears at the top of every league page, which is generous of them and an open invitation to spam.
