What we're proposing

Every result gets its own match card: the score as a scoreboard, each game with both players, their handicaps as separate tags and any breaks beside the player who made them, plus the venue and links to the other results that night.

Why

Today the closest thing is a weekly page listing every match that week one after another, with a "Brk" column that is empty for most rows. A match card gives a single result an address that can be shared in a team's WhatsApp group, and a century somewhere to be seen.

Covers idea 30. The team score is real (Cheveley Road (Thurs) 6, Sawston 3 4, Friday 2 October); the players, frames and breaks are sample data.
