What we're proposing

Rankings with a qualifying number of frames, set by the league and a choice of order: plus/minus, win percentage or highest break. A dash replaces the zero where a player has no break.

Why

On the current rankings a player with two games at 100% sits above a player with three at 66.67%, and dozens of players show a highest break of 0. Neither is wrong as such, but both make the table harder to believe early in a season.
