What we're proposing

A page written for league secretaries: what they get, how much of their week it takes, what it costs (with a calculator rather than worked examples), how payment works and a button to start. It finishes with the leagues already on the site and the link with the national governing body.

Why

The current Join page explains pricing in two paragraphs of examples, quotes a build value of £35,000 to £40,000, describes the administrator as "he" throughout and still has instructions for Internet Explorer 6. The offer itself is good and cheap. The page undersells it.

Covers ideas 17, 41, 42, 45 and 47. Prices are the current published rates.

How it works

  1. Before the season

    Set up once

    Add your clubs, teams and fixtures once. Tell us how your league scores and the site follows your rules.

  2. Each week

    Results in, everything updates

    Captains can submit results from their phone for you to approve, or you type them in from the match cards. For a league with four divisions it typically takes about an hour a week.

  3. All season

    Players keep up

    Players follow their team, get told when results are in and can look back at every frame they have played on the site.
