What we're proposing

Home captains submit the result from their phone at the end of the night, in four steps: which match, the frames, with each player chosen from the team's list (or added if they are new) and their break alongside, a photo of the paper match card, and a final check. The away captain is asked to confirm it, and it goes live once they do, or after 48 hours if nobody queries it. The secretary sees everything in one queue and only steps in when there's a query.

Why

The site already has a "Submit Result" link on unplayed fixtures, so the principle is established. Most of the secretary's hour a week goes on typing up match cards that someone else has already written out once. Moving that to the people holding the card, with a confirmation from the other side, takes the bulk of it away without losing the check.

Which match?

Matches with a result due and this week's fixtures.

Frames and breaks

Five games, best of two frames each. Choose each player from the team's list, which holds everyone who has played for the team this season. If someone is new, choose "Add a new name" and type it as written on the card. Put each player's highest break in the box beside their name, and leave it empty if there wasn't one of 20 or more.

Total

BehaviourOnce a name is picked it disappears from the other dropdowns on that side, so nobody can be entered twice. A new name is checked against the team's list, joins it for the team's next match, and is linked to a player record by the secretary when approving. The form follows the league's own format (games per match, frames per game, whether draws count) and the running total updates as you go.
